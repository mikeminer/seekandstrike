## English

**Title:** SEEK & STRIKE

**Subtitle (max 80):** Tactical wave-based shooter for Seeker: plant the charge, hold, win.

**Short description:**
Fast 6-minute rounds, squads of 5-6 soldiers, a charge to plant or defuse. Aim through the red dot, revive your squad,
throw grenades and climb the ranks.

**Full description:**
SEEK & STRIKE is a first-person shooter built for the Seeker.

- **Attack and defence:** attackers plant a charge on the objective, defenders hold out or defuse it.
  Reinforcements return in waves every few seconds.
- **Five classes:** Soldier, Medic, Engineer, Field Ops and Covert Ops, each with its own job.
- **Four maps:** East Quay, Salt Warehouses, Rail Yard and Trieste — Porto Vecchio, rebuilt from
  OpenStreetMap data.
- **Progression:** 50 levels and ranks, daily and weekly challenges, rifle finishes and dot colours.
  Rewards are cosmetic only: no advantage in a match.
- **Tuned touch controls:** separate look and aim sensitivity, adjustable aim assist, a one-minute
  tutorial.
- **One free match every day** against bots. The Daily Pass opens unlimited matches and online play;
  it is activated from the Seeker wallet.

**Keywords:** fps, shooter, tactical, multiplayer, seeker, solana, bots, offline
