FAQ

Which devices are supported?

SEEK & STRIKE is built for the Solana Seeker and runs on recent Android phones. It is distributed through the Solana dApp Store.

Can I play offline?

Yes: every map can be played offline against bots. One match a day is free; the Daily Pass unlocks unlimited matches and online play.

How does the leaderboard work?

The server scores every online round: 100 per takedown, 300 per win and 50 per round played. It resets every week (ISO week, UTC).

Is voice chat recorded?

No. The microphone opens only while you hold TALK and your voice is relayed in real time to your teammates only. Nothing is stored.

Is the game open source?

Yes, under GPL-3.0-or-later. The source code is on GitHub.
